# Level 5 Barbarian – character sheet

An easy level 5 **Human Barbarian (Path of the Berserker)** for a new D&D player (2024 rules).

**Open it online: https://mathiaslourenco.github.io/barbarian-character-sheet/** (no login needed, works on phone too)

- **[Barbarian-Character-Sheet.pdf](Barbarian-Character-Sheet.pdf)**: printable 2-page sheet. Page 1 is the character, page 2 explains everything you can do plus the rules in 30 seconds.
- **[Barbarian-Character-Sheet.html](Barbarian-Character-Sheet.html)**: interactive sheet with a RAGE button, attack rolls with all bonuses added, HP tracker, end-of-turn reminders and dice. It remembers your character in the browser of each device you use.
