# Level 5 Barbarian – character sheet

An easy level 5 **Human Barbarian (Path of the Berserker)** for a new D&D player (2024 rules).

- **[Barbarian-Character-Sheet.pdf](Barbarian-Character-Sheet.pdf)**: printable 2-page sheet. Page 1 is the character, page 2 explains everything you can do plus the rules in 30 seconds. Click it to view it right here on GitHub.
- **[Barbarian-Character-Sheet.html](Barbarian-Character-Sheet.html)**: interactive sheet with a RAGE button, attack rolls with all bonuses added, HP tracker, end-of-turn reminders and dice.
  To use it: click the file, then **Download raw file** (download icon, top right), and double-click the downloaded file. It opens in your browser and remembers your character on that device.
