Your turn in 5 steps

  1. Bonus action: RAGE. Only on your first turn. It keeps going as long as you keep attacking.
  2. Go Reckless before your first swing: advantage, plus Frenzy damage.
  3. Action: Attack. Swing your greataxe.
  4. Extra Attack: swing again! Your Attack action gives you 2 swings. Hit someone? You may also Cleave an enemy next to them.
  5. Move up to 40 ft: before, between or after your swings.

Notes

What you can do

Rage

Bonus action · 3 per long rest · 1 back on a short rest
  • Take half damage from bludgeoning, piercing & slashing (weapons, claws, bites).
  • +2 damage on your Strength attacks.
  • Advantage on Strength checks and saving throws.
  • Lasts until the end of your next turn and keeps going while you attack enemies (or use a bonus action to extend it). Max 10 minutes.
  • No spellcasting and no heavy armor while raging.

Reckless Attack

Your choice at your first attack each turn

Get advantage on your Strength attacks until your next turn. The catch: enemies get advantage against you too. Usually worth it, because you have lots of HP and Rage halves the damage.

Frenzy (Berserker)

While raging + reckless

The first enemy you hit on each of your turns takes an extra 2d6 damage. This is why you Rage and go Reckless every fight.

Extra Attack

Every time you take the Attack action

You attack twice instead of once: two greataxe swings, or two handaxe throws. You can walk between the two attacks, or aim them at different enemies.

Savage Attacker (feat)

Once per turn, when you hit

Roll your weapon's damage dice twice and use the higher roll.

Weapon Mastery

Always on
  • Greataxe – Cleave: after a hit, make one extra attack on a 2nd enemy within 5 ft of the first (no +4 on that damage). Once per turn.
  • Handaxe – Vex: after a hit, you have advantage on your next attack against that enemy.
  • You have one more mastery slot. Pick it when you find a new weapon you like.

Tough Body

Always on
  • Unarmored Defense: AC 14 (10 + DEX + CON) with no armor.
  • Danger Sense: advantage on Dexterity saves (traps, explosions, fireballs).
  • Fast Movement: speed 40 ft (if you don't wear heavy armor).

Primal Knowledge

While raging

Acrobatics, Intimidation, Perception, Stealth and Survival can be rolled with Strength instead, and Rage gives Strength checks advantage. Need to scare someone? Rage first.

Human

Species traits
  • Heroic Inspiration (1 per long rest): rolled badly? Reroll one die and use the new roll.
  • Tough (feat): +10 max HP, already counted in your 65.
  • Skillful: trained in Insight (spot lies and moods).
  • No darkvision: carry a torch in dark places.

Backstory (an idea)

You spent twelve years with a mercenary company as the one who kicked down the door. When the company broke up, you kept the axe, the dice, and a bad habit of solving problems head-first.

  • Personality: loud, loyal, always hungry. Greets strangers like old friends.
  • Ideal: nobody gets left behind.
  • Bond: the old company banner, tied around the axe handle.
  • Flaw: charges first, plans later.

Rules in 30 seconds

  • Advantage: roll two d20s, keep the higher. Disadvantage: keep the lower.
  • Natural 20 on an attack: always hits and it's a critical. Roll double the damage dice.
  • Natural 1 on an attack: always a miss.
  • 0 HP: you fall unconscious. Each turn roll a d20: 10+ is a success, 3 successes = stable, 3 failures = dead. A natural 20 gets you back up with 1 HP.
  • Short rest (1 hour): 1 Rage back, spend Hit Dice to heal (d12+3 each).
  • Long rest (8 hours): everything back to full, including Heroic Inspiration.
